# 核心角色组 v3 Prompt

## 成人立绘基准

目标：显著拉开两名男性角色，不仅换颜色和道具，还改变脸型、眉眼、发型、肩线、服装轮廓和姿态。

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: revised production adult character lineup for a Chinese romance productivity game
Input images: Image 1 is the v2 lineup used only to preserve the overall colored-pencil watercolor medium, general palette families, character order and signature props. Redesign the two men's facial structure, hair silhouette, clothing silhouette and posture to make them unmistakably different. Image 2 is the valley world-style reference.

Shendu: tall, slim and long-lined; calm long-oval face, softer jaw, straight low eyebrows, gentle slightly downturned eyes, reserved mouth; smooth dark hair pulled into a neat low knot at the nape with forehead mostly clear and no dramatic loose side lock; gray-green hip-length short jacket with clean rectangular hem over moon-white shirt; narrow sleeves; straight relaxed shoulders; bamboo pole vertical beside him; quiet frontal posture.

Guwan: medium-tall lean athletic build with visibly broader shoulders than Shendu; wider angular face, defined jaw, strongly arched eyebrows, sharper slightly upturned eyes, wry asymmetric mouth; dark hair in a high messy knot with one unmistakable long side lock; smoke-brown scarf creating a high collar; terracotta-red long split travel coat reaching near the knees; closed ochre umbrella across one shoulder; weight on one leg and torso angled away.

Taotao: adult woman with warm oval face, lively rounded eyes, two compact looped buns, honey-yellow top, rouge apron, copper ladle and rabbit sugar painting; retain her established friendly adult design.

All characters use 6–6.5-head adult proportions. Refined colored pencil over transparent watercolor, soft dark-brown contours, warm handmade paper. Full bodies, equal baseline, plain paper background.
Constraints: exactly three adults; preserve order Shendu, Guwan, Taotao; Shendu and Guwan must not share face shape, eyebrow shape, eye shape, hair-knot height, coat length, shoulder line or resting pose; no facial hair; no text or labels.
Avoid: same-face syndrome, matching top knots, matching jawlines, matching coats, chibi proportions, stocky bodies, child faces, anime card rendering, xianxia clothing, armor, modern fashion, extra props, scenery, watermark.
```

## 山谷 Q 版转译基准

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: simplified distant chibi character lineup for an interactive valley scene
Input images: Image 1 is the mandatory adult identity reference. Image 2 is the mandatory valley style and scale context.
Primary request: Translate the three adults into a separate low-detail 3-head-tall chibi system for display at approximately 56–80 CSS pixels high. Do not shrink the adult drawings. Redesign faces as gentle picture-book chibi faces: rounder face, tiny simple nose and mouth, larger but restrained dot-like eyes, one clear brow gesture. Keep them recognizable through silhouette, palette, pose and prop.

Shendu keeps: neat low-knot silhouette, gray-green short jacket block, moon-white chest triangle, vertical bamboo pole, straight quiet pose. Use level brows and calm drooping eyes.
Guwan keeps: high messy knot plus one long side lock, terracotta long-coat block, dark scarf triangle, closed umbrella over one shoulder, angled skeptical pose. Use one raised brow and narrow sideways eyes.
Taotao keeps: two looped buns, honey-yellow top plus rouge apron block, copper ladle or simple solid rabbit-sugar silhouette, lively forward lean. Use rounded cheerful eyes.

Simplification: flat watercolor color blocks with only a few colored-pencil contour marks; no fabric patterns, no individual fingers, no hair strands, no detailed noses, no adult jaw anatomy, no garment micro-folds, no umbrella-rib detail, no sugar internal pattern.
Composition: exactly three isolated full-body chibi figures at equal scale, generous separation, plain warm paper background, no scenery.
Constraints: visibly the same three characters but not adult faces pasted onto short bodies; readable at thumbnail size; no text, labels, border, UI or watermark.
Avoid: adult realistic faces, beard, caricature, baby/toddler look, oversized sparkling anime eyes, dense detail, same silhouette, 3D, modern clothes, extra props.
```
