```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: landscape in-game relationship event illustration for Productivity Valley, 3:2 composition
Input image: the reference lineup establishes exact character identity, outfit, hairstyle, adult proportions, colored-pencil storybook linework and muted palette.
Style/medium: warm Chinese colored-pencil storybook illustration on lightly fibrous cream paper, clear hand-drawn contours, restrained watercolor wash, mature and gentle rather than childish.
Composition/framing: landscape medium or medium-wide shot; the character’s face, hands and signature prop remain readable at small UI size and away from crop edges.
Player viewpoint: never establish a fixed player appearance. Imply the player only through a cropped hand, sleeve or empty place in the near foreground.
Color palette: warm cream paper, muted earth colors and restrained character-specific accents; low saturation.
Constraints: exact continuity with the reference; adult natural body proportions; one visible complete character only; no text, frame, UI or watermark.
Avoid: anime card art, photorealism, 3D render, chibi proportions, xianxia fantasy, neon, purple-pink glow, ornate costume, modern objects, extra fingers and duplicated props.
```
