# 山谷风格候选 Prompt

三个方向共享同一个世界、色板和构图要求，只改变媒介侧重点。

## 方向 A：水彩绘本

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: wide environment concept for the main interactive scene of a cozy productivity game
Primary request: A quiet, welcoming Chinese countryside valley with one small expandable cottage. A pale winding footpath enters from the bottom and leads to the cottage. Low hills, scattered trees, bamboo, grasses, a few wildflowers, a small stream and distant layered mountains create a gentle lived-in valley. The scene should feel like completing a real task makes one cottage window glow.
Style/medium: soft traditional watercolor picture-book illustration with restrained colored-pencil details, subtle dark-brown hand-drawn contours, warm handmade paper grain
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2; cottage centered slightly above the middle; path clearly visible; distinct foreground, midground and background; natural open pockets around the yard and path for up to twelve tiny clickable characters; two clearly readable cottage windows and one door; modular side space for future room extensions
Lighting/mood: diffuse late-spring morning light, peaceful, intimate, hopeful, gently inhabited
Color palette: warm paper cream, moss green, deep leaf green, terracotta roof, wood brown, moon-white sky, muted indigo distance, tiny rouge flowers and lamplight yellow accents
Constraints: no people, no animals, no text, no UI, no frame, no watermark; Chinese rural vernacular, not a famous real place; readable at tablet size
Avoid: neon, purple-pink gradient, xianxia palace, pagoda skyline, golden magic, photorealism, 3D render, anime card art, hard black shadows, dense clutter
```

## 方向 B：纸片拼贴

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: modular environment concept for the main interactive scene of a cozy productivity game
Primary request: A quiet Chinese countryside valley built like a dimensional picture-book paper diorama. One small expandable cottage sits above a pale winding footpath. Low hills, trees, bamboo, grasses, wildflowers, a narrow stream and distant mountains form clearly separable visual layers.
Style/medium: watercolor-painted paper-cut collage, gently raised paper edges, subtle handmade fiber texture, restrained colored-pencil accents and soft dark-brown outlines; handcrafted rather than digital-flat
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2; cottage centered slightly above middle; strong readable path; foreground, ground plane, cottage, vegetation and distant hills visibly separable; open pockets for twelve tiny clickable characters; two readable windows and one door; clear attachment areas for future room modules
Lighting/mood: soft diffuse afternoon, warm, calm, tactile, domestic, hopeful
Color palette: warm paper cream, moss and olive green, deep leaf green, terracotta orange, wood-ink brown, muted indigo, small rouge and lamplight yellow accents
Constraints: no people, no animals, no text, no UI, no frame, no watermark; all paper layers share one coherent perspective; Chinese rural vernacular, not fantasy architecture
Avoid: scrapbook stickers, childish classroom craft, neon, purple gradient, xianxia ornament, palace, photorealism, glossy 3D, anime card art, hard shadows, excessive depth-of-field blur
```

## 方向 C：彩铅故事书

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: readable environment concept for the main interactive scene of a cozy productivity game
Primary request: A small Chinese countryside valley and expandable cottage, drawn for a richly illustrated storybook. A pale winding path enters from below, passes open grassy pockets and reaches the cottage. Include low hills, a few clear tree and bamboo silhouettes, a narrow stream, wildflowers and distant mountains without clutter.
Style/medium: colored-pencil storybook drawing over light transparent watercolor, visible gentle pencil strokes, warm paper surface, soft broken dark-brown contours; clear shapes at small screen size
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2; cottage centered slightly above middle; path and cottage are the strongest shapes; deliberate negative-space pockets for twelve tiny clickable characters; two clear windows and one door; room-extension space on both sides; foreground elements do not block the path
Lighting/mood: mild late-spring daylight, cozy, grounded, quiet and encouraging
Color palette: warm cream paper, moss green, deep leaf green, terracotta orange, wood brown, moon white and muted indigo, minimal rouge flowers and yellow window accents
Constraints: no people, no animals, no text, no UI, no frame, no watermark; Chinese countryside rather than Japanese or European village; readable silhouettes and moderate detail
Avoid: neon, saturated purple or pink, xianxia, grand architecture, photorealism, 3D, anime, heavy ink, black shadows, overly busy vegetation
```
